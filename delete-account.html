Delete Your Account

How to permanently delete your PRIVE account and the data associated with it.
Last updated: 3 September 2026

You can ask us to delete your PRIVE account at any time, and you do not need to have the app installed to do so. We handle every request ourselves and confirm by email when it is done.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to prive.support@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line Account deletion.
  3. In the message, include the phone number registered to your PRIVE account, so we can identify it.

Email us to delete my account

We will contact you on the phone number or email address on the account to confirm the request came from you before anything is deleted.

What happens next

  • We acknowledge your request within 3 business days.
  • After we verify that the request is yours, the account is deleted within 30 days.
  • We email you a confirmation once deletion is complete.
  • Deletion is permanent. Your account cannot be restored afterwards, and any active subscription benefits are lost.

Cancelling a subscription is not the same as deleting your account

If you only want to stop being billed, cancel your subscription in the app under Subscription, or through Google Play or the App Store if you subscribed there. Deleting your account does not automatically cancel a subscription billed by Google or Apple — cancel it in the relevant store as well.

Deleting individual items instead

If you do not want to delete your whole account, you can remove your profile photo and individual reviews directly in the app, and turn off any notification category you do not want.

Questions

Write to prive.support@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
