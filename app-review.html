App Review Information

PRIVE for iOS — information for the App Store review team.
Last updated: 19 September 2026

Direct link: prive-demo.mp4

2. Purpose and target audience

PRIVE is a membership-based dining and lifestyle discovery app for Azerbaijan. Members receive a personal QR code that grants a fixed discount at partner restaurants, cafes, hotels, spas and shops.

Problem it solves: discount offers here are scattered across paper cards, social media posts and separate venue programmes, and are hard to find, verify and use; venues have no reliable way to reach guests or measure an offer. PRIVE brings verified partner venues, their current offers and one in-person redemption method into a single app.

Audience: adults living in or visiting Azerbaijan, primarily Baku, who eat out regularly. Not directed at children.

3. Setup and access

Sign-in is by phone number with a one-time code. A permanent demo account with a fixed code is configured, so no SMS needs to be received:

Phone: +994 XX XXX XX XX
Code: XXXXXX

There is only one account type and this account has full access. The app is in Azerbaijani (Profil > Dil). No sample files are needed.

  • Sign in: “Daxil ol” > demo number > “Kod göndər” > fixed code > “Doğrula”. New users tap “Qeydiyyatdan keç”, which uses the same phone and OTP step.
  • Home (“Əsas”): nearby venues and daily selections. Location is optional; the app works fully if it is denied.
  • Search (“Axtarış”): by venue, cuisine or category, with filters for distance, discount and rating.
  • Venue detail: About / Reviews / Photos / Menu tabs and “Masa rezerv et” to reserve a table. Reservations: Profil > Rezervasiyalarım.
  • Membership QR code: the centre button in the tab bar; venue staff scan it to apply the discount. History: Profil > Tarixçə.
  • Reels: short venue presentations that can be liked, saved and shared.
  • Assistant (“Luna”): answers questions about venues and app features, from the home screen button or Profil > Yardım və dəstək.
  • Reporting: any review can be reported from the “…” menu on the review. Reports are listed under Profil > Şikayətlərim and are handled by our moderation team within 24 hours.
  • Account deletion: Profil > “Hesabı sil” deletes the account and all associated personal data after a confirmation prompt, in app.

4. External services

  • Our own backend service, operated by us: stores and serves all app content — accounts, partner venues, offers, reservations, reviews and QR redemptions. It is the only backend the app uses for core functionality.
  • SMS provider, called only by our backend, solely to deliver the one-time sign-in code.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google LLC): push notifications.
  • OpenStreetMap map tiles: map display and venue locations. No Google Maps services are used.
  • Apple Core Location: while-in-use only, to sort venues by distance. Optional.
  • Apple Speech framework and system text-to-speech: on-device voice input and speech output. No third-party AI or LLM provider is used and no voice data is shared.
  • Payment processors: none. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases and no payment flow; all features are free.

5. Regional differences

The app functions consistently across all regions: no region-locked features, no regional content differences and no regional pricing — PRIVE is free everywhere. All partner venues are physically located in Azerbaijan (currently Baku), so a user abroad sees the same venues and screens with larger distances. The interface is offered in Azerbaijani in all regions.

6. Regulated industry and third-party material

PRIVE does not operate in a highly regulated industry: it is not a financial, medical, gambling or government service, it processes no payments and handles no regulated data. All venue names, logos, photographs, menus and videos belong to our partner venues and are published under signed partnership agreements that authorise PRIVE to display their content and offer the stated discount. No other protected third-party material is included. Copies of the agreements can be provided on request.

The submitted build has been tested on physical devices running the latest iOS.
